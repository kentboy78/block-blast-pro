📲 Use as an iPhone App

This game is built as a full-screen iOS Standalone Web App. Adding a shortcut to your iPhone Home Screen hides Safari’s address bar, enables offline play, and gives it a native app icon!

  1. 1
    Open in Safari on your iPhone

    Access this game via your Netlify or GitHub Pages HTTPS link in iOS Safari.

  2. 2
    Tap the Share button ⬆️

    Located at the bottom center of Safari's toolbar.

  3. 3
    Tap “Add to Home Screen” ➕

    Confirm the name Block Blast+ and tap Add. Launch it from your Home Screen just like a native iOS app!
